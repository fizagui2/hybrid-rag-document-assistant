# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Requests must be submitted to a direct manager at least 48 hours in advance. Remote work requires a stable internet connection and a company-issued VPN client, as outlined in the IT Security Policy.

# Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days per year. Unused vacation days roll over into the following year, up to a maximum of 30 days. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

# Expense Reimbursement

Employees may submit expense reports for approved business travel, client meals, and work-related supplies. Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of submission. Expense reports must include itemized receipts for any single expense over $25.

# Parental Leave

Full-time employees are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Leave must be requested through HR at least 30 days in advance when possible.

# Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Managers are required to provide written feedback covering goals, achievements, and areas for growth.
