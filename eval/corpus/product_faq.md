# Pricing Tiers

The product is offered in three tiers: Starter ($9/month), Professional ($29/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). The Starter tier supports up to 3 team members; Professional supports up to 20.

# Support Hours

Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 support through a dedicated Slack channel.

# Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of their initial purchase. Refunds are not available for the final month of an annual subscription once more than 14 days have passed.

# Data Export

Customers on any tier can export their data in CSV or JSON format at any time from the account settings page. Data exports are processed immediately and do not require support intervention.

# Account Cancellation

Accounts can be canceled at any time from the billing settings page. Canceling an account does not issue a refund for the current billing period, but access continues until the end of the paid period.
