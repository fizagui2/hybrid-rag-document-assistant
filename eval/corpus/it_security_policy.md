# Password Requirements

All employee accounts require passwords of at least 12 characters, including at least one number and one symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and cannot repeat the previous 5 passwords used.

# Remote Access Security

Employees connecting to internal systems from outside the office must use the company-issued VPN client. Split-tunneling is disabled on all VPN connections to ensure traffic is routed securely.

# Device Encryption

All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before being used for work. IT verifies encryption status during the device setup process.

# Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication is required for all accounts with access to production systems or customer data. Employees may use an authenticator app or a hardware security key.

# Data Classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, or Confidential. Confidential data may not be stored on personal devices or shared via personal email accounts.
